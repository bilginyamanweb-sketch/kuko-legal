This policy describes exactly what KUKO AI Fitness does with your data. Every statement here corresponds to behaviour enforced in the app's code, not to an intention.

1. Summary

  • Your profile, food diary, workouts, favourites and reminder times are stored on your device.
  • Meal photos are analysed on your device when an on-device model is available. If it cannot identify the dish, the photo may be sent to our analysis service — see §4.
  • Health data is read only after you grant permission and switch sync on, and only daily totals are kept.
  • Signing in is optional and does not upload anything — cloud backup is not implemented in this version. Every feature works without an account.
  • Ads are shown. Personalised ads are off unless you turn them on.
  • You can erase everything from Settings → Privacy and data → Delete all my data.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share health data with advertisers.

3. Camera and photo library

Requested only at the moment you use the scan feature. Declining leaves every other part of the app fully usable.

  • The photo is used solely to estimate what the dish is and its nutrition.
  • The app does not keep the photo after analysis.
  • The app does not browse your photo library; you pick a single image.

Nutrition figures are estimates, not measurements. They are not medical, clinical, or dietary advice, and must not be used to manage a medical condition. If you have a health condition, an eating disorder, are pregnant, or have specific dietary requirements, consult a qualified professional.

4. How meal photos are analysed

Two paths, in this order:

On-device (preferred). When an on-device recognition model is present, the photo is analysed locally. It never leaves your phone and no network connection is used.

Our analysis service (fallback). If there is no on-device model, or the on-device model cannot identify the dish, the photo may be sent over an encrypted connection to our analysis service, which uses Google's Gemini API to identify it. This fallback also has a shared daily limit; once it is reached for the day, the app asks you to add the meal manually instead of sending the photo anywhere.

In that case:

  • Only the image and its format are sent. No name, email, account identifier, health data or diary content is attached.
  • Our service does not store the image. It forwards the request, returns the result, and discards it.
  • Google processes the image as a data processor under the Google APIs Terms of Service and the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service. This app currently uses the free tier of the Gemini API. Under Google's terms, content submitted to the free tier may be used by Google to develop and improve its products, including training its models — unlike the paid tier, which Google states is excluded from such use. We do not attach any identifying information to what is sent, but the image content itself is subject to this. If we move to the paid tier, this section will be updated before that takes effect, and the free-tier disclosure will be removed as it will no longer apply.
  • Your IP address is visible to our service and to Google as an unavoidable part of making an internet request. We use it only for abuse rate-limiting and do not link it to your profile.

If you would rather no photo ever leaves your device or is subject to Google's free-tier terms, do not use the scan feature — you can log every meal manually, and the app is fully functional that way.

5. Health data (Health Connect / Apple Health)

Off by default. If you switch it on and grant the platform permission, the app reads:

  • step count,
  • active energy burned,
  • workout sessions.

What happens to it:

  • Only daily totals are stored on your device. Individual samples are read, summed, and discarded.
  • It is used solely to display your activity on your dashboard.
  • It is never sold, never shared with advertisers, never used for advertising or analytics, and never transmitted to us.
  • If you allow it, workouts you complete in the app can be written back to your health store.

Withdraw access at any time in the app, or in Health Connect / Apple Health settings. Turning it off in the app also deletes the locally cached totals.

This use complies with the Health Connect Permissions policy and Apple's HealthKit requirements: health data is used only for the user-facing fitness features described here.

6. Google account (optional)

If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google, and show them in the app.

Cloud backup is not implemented in this version. Signing in does not upload your diary, workouts, profile or favourites anywhere — all of it stays on your device. Sign-in currently only links the account for a backup feature planned for a later release. When that ships, this policy will be updated before it is switched on, and it will be opt-in.

  • Sign-in is never required; every feature works without it.
  • Authentication tokens are handled by Google's official SDK in platform secure storage. The app does not read or retain them.
  • Signing out ends the session. Your local data stays on your device.

Retention: the name, email and photo are held only in the app's local session and are discarded when you sign out. We operate no server that stores them. To be certain nothing remains, sign out and uninstall, or use Settings → Privacy and data → Delete all my data.

7. Advertising

The app is free and supported by ads served through Google AdMob.

  • Non-personalised ads are served by default.
  • Personalised ads require you to opt in at Settings → Privacy and data. When enabled, Google may use your device's advertising identifier.
  • On iOS 14.5+, personalised ads also require the App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • Turning personalisation off does not remove ads; it makes them non-personalised.
  • Rewarded ads are optional and only ever grant additional features.

We never send your health data, diary content or profile to advertising networks. Google's handling is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

8. Analytics

Not currently collected. The app contains an opt-in analytics toggle that is off by default and no analytics SDK is integrated in this version. If that changes, this policy will be updated before the change ships, and the toggle will remain opt-in.

11. Security

  • All network traffic uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • API keys are never embedded in the app; the analysis service holds its credentials server-side.
  • Local data lives in app-private storage that other apps cannot read.
  • Release builds are minified and strip debug logging so personal data cannot leak into device logs.

No system is perfectly secure. If you discover a vulnerability, please report it to support@gifgifstudio.com.

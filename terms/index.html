3. Estimates, AI and accuracy

Parts of the App use automated recognition — an on-device model and, as a fallback, a service built on Google's Gemini API — to guess what a dish is from a photo and to estimate its nutrition.

  • These are estimates and they will sometimes be wrong, sometimes substantially: the same dish varies by recipe, portion, oil and preparation, and a photo cannot show what is inside it.
  • The App shows a warning when recognition confidence is low, but a confident answer is not a guaranteed-correct one.
  • Always review and correct what the App fills in before saving it, and edit portion sizes to match what you actually ate.
  • Step counts and calories burned come from your device's health store or platform sensors. Their accuracy is determined by that platform and hardware, not by us.
  • Recipe, exercise and nutrition content is provided for general information. We do not warrant that any figure, ingredient list or nutrition value is accurate, complete or current.

Decisions you make on the basis of these estimates are your own. Do not use them where accuracy matters medically — for example, to calculate insulin doses or to manage a clinically prescribed diet.
